Tools to learn, draw and begin again

School and creative materials for 1,500 children affected by the earthquakes in Venezuela. The first 600 kits are now moving from plan to reality.

0% of the campaign goalUpdated:
First batch underway

600 children. One practical, reusable kit each.

About USD 2,700 is available on the ground after transfer fees and exchange-rate costs. Purchases are being coordinated in Caracas with the Academia de Ciencias, UCV volunteers and Creative Commons Venezuela.

The first-batch kit

Useful · durable · ready for school

Made to be used, not discarded

  • 1 locally made waterproof pencil case
  • 12 premium 4 mm coloured pencils
  • 4 premium HB graphite pencils
  • 1 simple metal sharpener
  • 1 large drawing pad with 20 blank, bound sheets
Outer bags are not charged to the kit budget: we are seeking an in-kind donation so every available dollar stays with the children’s materials.
